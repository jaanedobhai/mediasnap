📱 iPhone & iPad Share Sheet Integration

MediaSnap iOS Shortcut

Download any Video, Audio, or Image directly from the iOS Share Sheet on YouTube, Instagram, Reddit, TikTok, Facebook, Twitter, Threads & Pinterest.

1 Open Shortcuts App on iPhone

Open the built-in Shortcuts app on your iPhone or iPad, tap + in the top right to create a new shortcut, and name it MediaSnap Downloader.

2 Enable Share Sheet Access

Tap the (i) Info icon at the bottom of the screen and toggle "Show in Share Sheet" to ON. Select Receive URLs, Web Pages & Text.

3 Add API Actions

Add the following actions in order:

A. Get Contents of URL (Fetch Info):

https://mediasnap-production-3e80.up.railway.app/api/info?url=[Shortcut Input]

B. Choose from Menu (Select Type & Quality):

  • 🎬 Video: Prompt quality selection (4K / 1080p / 720p / Best).
  • 🎧 Audio MP3: Download 320kbps MP3 track.
  • 🖼 Image: Download original high-res image/thumbnail.

C. Get Contents of URL (Stream File Download):

https://mediasnap-production-3e80.up.railway.app/api/download?url=[Shortcut Input]&formatId=[Selected Format]

D. Save File:

Add the Save File action to automatically save the downloaded video/audio into your iPhone's Files / Downloads folder or Camera Roll!

4 Try it Out Anywhere!

Open YouTube, Instagram, Reddit, TikTok, Facebook, Twitter, or Pinterest → tap Share → select MediaSnap Downloader → choose Video (4K/1080p), Audio, or Image → Done! 🎉
