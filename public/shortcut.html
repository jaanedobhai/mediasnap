iPhone & iPad

MediaSnap
iOS Shortcut

Download videos, audio and images directly from the iOS Share Sheet — no app switching, no copy-pasting.

Add to iPhone & Siri Tap on your iPhone → Shortcuts app opens automatically
1
Tap the button above on your iPhone

The Shortcuts app opens automatically and shows an "Add Shortcut" prompt. Tap it — you're done. No manual setup needed.

2
Open any supported app

Open YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, X, Pinterest, Facebook or any other app and find a video, audio or image you want.

▶ YouTube 📸 Instagram 🎵 TikTok 💬 Reddit 𝕏 Twitter / X 🎬 Facebook 📌 Pinterest 🧵 Threads
3
Tap Share → MediaSnap Downloader

Tap the Share button (the box-with-arrow icon) → scroll down → tap MediaSnap Downloader.

4
Choose type and quality

A menu appears — pick 🎬 Video, 🎧 Audio (MP3) or 🖼️ Image. For video, choose your quality (Best · 1080p · 720p · 480p · 360p). The file saves to your Files app automatically. 🎉

Manual API Setup

If you prefer to build the shortcut yourself in the Shortcuts app, use these API endpoints.

1. Fetch media info
https://mediasnap-eyhq.onrender.com/api/info?url=[Shortcut Input]
2. Download media
https://mediasnap-eyhq.onrender.com/api/download?url=[URL]&formatId=[Format]&title=[Title]
