iPhone & iPad

MediaSnap
iOS Shortcut

Official Apple-signed shortcut with native Share Sheet support. Download videos straight to Photos app in 1 tap.

Add to iPhone & Siri Tap on your iPhone → Shortcuts app opens automatically to install
1
Tap 'Add to iPhone & Siri' above

Your iPhone will open the Shortcuts app and display MediaSnap (with the filmstrip media icon). Tap Add Shortcut.

2
Open any app (Instagram, YouTube, TikTok...)

Go to any video, reel, post, or pin on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, X (Twitter), Facebook, or Pinterest.

▶ YouTube 📸 Instagram 🎵 TikTok 💬 Reddit 𝕏 Twitter / X 🎬 Facebook 📌 Pinterest
3
Tap Share → MediaSnap

In the iOS Share menu, scroll down and tap MediaSnap. (Or say "Hey Siri, MediaSnap" to download a copied link!)

4
Choose format & Done!

A native menu appears: pick 🎬 Video (Best / 1080p / 720p), 🎧 Audio (MP3), or 🖼️ Image. Videos and images save directly into your iPhone's Photos app (Camera Roll)! 🎉
